# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive project community.

Expected behavior: be respectful, discuss ideas in good faith, accept constructive feedback, focus criticism on work rather than people, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub contact options.
